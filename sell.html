Step 1 — Register as a seller

One time, two minutes. Real name, real contact, real town — that’s the price of admission, and it’s what keeps the scammers out. If you won’t say who you are, you can’t sell here.

Why we do it this way

Facebook Marketplace makes you have an account — same idea. Every seller gets a public profile with star ratings and buyer reviews, so everyone knows who they’re dealing with.

Register once and you can list as much as you want. Your first listing can go in the form below right after.

Step 2 — List your item

Registered? List your item in about a minute — like Facebook Marketplace, without the flakes.

How it works

1. Fill out the form below — photos attach right here, nothing to email separately.

2. Hit List my item. No approval queue — if it’s legal, it goes up, usually in a day or two.

3. You set the asking price. Pickup, shipping, or both — say so in the description. Your info rides with every listing, and anyone who scams a buyer answers for it — see the vendor terms.

Snap a few pictures — they come straight to us with your listing. No separate email needed.
