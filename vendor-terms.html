Vendor terms

The door is wide open — and there’s a camera on it. Here’s the deal for selling on Pour Dawgz.

1. Anyone can sell

No approval queue, no gatekeeping. If it’s legal, list it — handmade goods, tools, parts, art, soap, whatever you sell. You set your price.

2. Nobody sells anonymous

To open a booth you verify who you are: real name, address, phone, and a payout account in the same name. No verified identity, no booth. Scammers run on anonymity — not here.

3. New sellers get a hold

First sales sit on hold about two weeks until buyers confirm delivery. Buyer gets their stuff, then you get paid. In that order.

The scammer policy

Zero tolerance. You scam one of our buyers and this is what happens — all of it, in order:

Banned for life

Instant, permanent, no appeal. And since your booth is tied to your verified identity, you don’t get to just open a new one.

Buyers get refunded

Your held funds go straight to making buyers whole. You don’t profit from the scam — you fund the refunds.

The wall

Your business name goes on our public banned-seller wall, right here on the site, so everybody knows what you did.

Reported

Real fraud gets reported — the FBI’s internet crime unit, the state attorney general, and your payment processor. A processor fraud flag follows you everywhere, not just here.

Bottom line: honest sellers get an open door and a fair shake. Crooks get their world turned upside down — legally, thoroughly, and permanently.

These terms cover online booths. Physical flea market booths are free for the first 100 vendors, then $10 flat entry.
